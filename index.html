01 / ABOUT

Science across
scales.

I study how water, climate, and land systems interact — and how geospatial data can help us understand what is changing.

I am a PhD candidate in Geography at the University of Florida, working at the intersection of surface-water and groundwater hydrology, satellite remote sensing, watershed modeling, and geospatial artificial intelligence. My research connects field observations with Earth-observation products and data-driven models to study drought, groundwater quality, water storage, and land-use impacts.

My work spans case studies in Ethiopia, South Africa, and the United States. I also bring experience in university teaching, hydrologic engineering, and interdisciplinary collaboration.
