01 / ABOUT

Science across scales.

Connecting field measurements, models, and Earth observations to understand environmental change.

I am a hydrologist, remote sensing and geospatial scientist, and a Ph.D. candidate in Geography (Hydrologic Sciences) at the University of Florida. My research integrates hydrological and hydraulic modeling, satellite Earth observations, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), GIS, and artificial intelligence to investigate surface-water and groundwater systems, basin and watershed processes, land-use and land-cover change, and environmental change.

My work connects water-resources engineering, hydrological sciences, and geospatial technology, with research applications in Ethiopia, South Africa, and the United States.
