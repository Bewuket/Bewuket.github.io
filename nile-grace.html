Coverage and interpretation

  • Requested timeline: March 2002 to December 2024 (274 calendar months and 274 video frames).
  • Available modeled raster slices: 229 months within May 2003 to December 2024.
  • Unavailable months: 45 calendar months (including March 2002–April 2003). Missing months are not independent GRACE predictions.
  • Spatial context: Terrain is a visualization aid. Any outside-basin DEM fills are display-only, not reconstructed basin storage observations.
  • Research status: Preliminary derived prediction visualization. It should not be interpreted as direct high-resolution GRACE observations or independent spatial validation.

The website upload does not resample, edit, or regenerate the research rasters. Refer to the source analysis for methods, uncertainty, and missing-month display policy.
